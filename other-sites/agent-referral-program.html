Questions

Frequently Asked

Everything you need to know about how rates are assigned and when you get paid.

How exactly are the 10%, 15%, and 20% rates assigned?

Each rate is based on the order you referred the property — not how many you have active at any given time. Your 1st referred property always earns 10%, your 2nd always earns 15%, and your 3rd and every one after always earns 20%. These rates are permanent and do not change.

If a property I referred leaves management, does my referral order reset?

No. Your referral order is based on the sequence in which you referred properties, not on how many are currently active. If your 2nd referral leaves management, your next new referral is still counted as your 3rd (or higher) and earns 20%.

When do I start getting paid?

Your commission begins the first full month after the referred property goes active under Eagle Property Management. Payments are issued monthly on the same cycle as owner disbursements.

How long do I continue earning on a property?

For as long as the property remains under Eagle's active management. There is no expiration date — your commission continues month after month with no action required on your part.

Is there a limit to how many properties I can refer?

No limit at all. Every property from your 3rd referral onward earns the maximum rate of 20% per month — so the more you refer, the more passive income you build.

Who is eligible to participate?

Any licensed real estate agent or REALTOR® in Texas. You don't need to be at a specific brokerage — just register with Manuel, make the introduction, and Eagle handles everything from there.
