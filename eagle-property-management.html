Legal Protection

Eviction Services — We Handle It All

While evictions are uncommon, when they do occur, we manage the entire legal process on your behalf — from first notice to final possession. You never have to set foot in a courtroom.

01

Eviction Notice

Proper legal notice served to the tenant in compliance with Texas Property Code, establishing the official start of the eviction timeline.

02

Eviction Service — Court Appearance

We represent your interests at the Justice of the Peace court, presenting the case professionally so you don't need to appear.

03

Court Appeals

If a tenant appeals the ruling, we continue managing the process through the County Court at Law to protect your rights as a property owner.

04

Writ of Possession

We coordinate with the constable to obtain and execute the Writ of Possession, returning your property to your control quickly and lawfully.

You Shouldn't Have to Fight Alone

Navigating Texas eviction law is complex and time-consuming. One misstep can cost weeks and hundreds of dollars. Our team knows the process inside and out — and handles every step for you.

From proper notice delivery to final writ execution, Eagle Property Management protects your investment and your peace of mind.

About the Owner

Led by a Houston Real Estate Expert

Manuel Mata Jr. is a licensed REALTOR® and the broker/owner of Eagle Property Management LLC, a Houston-based property management company built on a foundation of trust, expertise, and genuine care for both property owners and tenants.

With deep roots in the Greater Houston real estate community, Manuel also serves as VP of Recruitment at White Picket Realty, where he leads strategic growth, agent training, and brokerage development. His dual experience gives Eagle's clients a rare combination of operational expertise and market insight.

A proud alumnus of the Texas REALTORS Leadership Program (TRLP) and an active board member of NAHREP North Houston, Manuel is committed to elevating the standard of professional real estate service throughout Houston. His work is guided by a simple belief: your investment should work for you — not the other way around.
